Who we are

Ivah Sound

Ivah Sound is a sound system and event production collective established in Helsinki 2010. As a collective they combine different styles, traditions and modes of production in order to provide awareness and interest in sound system culture; an important but often forgotten foundational movement in electronic music. Ivah Sound traces a direct link between the roots of sound system culture in Jamaica and the contemporary electronic bass music scene.

Focused around a custom-built 20 kilowatt system; Ivah Sound powers a community and represents sound system culture both in Finland and beyond.

Cheeta Horn

The Cheetah Horn is built around the legendary JBL 2380A, giving the cabinets a warm and true Hi Fi high end sound. The cabinets are designed by Mikko Laurila and built with the best Finnish birch plywood. The Cheetah Horns provides the sound with clear highs from 1-20 kHz ensuring a crisp and clear over all sound system.

MT121

MT121 redesigned by Mikko Laurila give the cabinet a robust construction. The MT121 is built with the best Finnish birch plywood and loaded with Fane Colossus 12MB giving the cabinet a clear and crisp mid sound with a kick on the low end and clearness in the high. The separate top section consisting of 2” coaxial drivers and horns gives clear and crisp highs!

Kick

Our kick low mid upper bass section consists of a new design by Mikko Laurila, built to sit on top of the Polar Bear Scoops. These speakers gives an precise and hard hitting kick. Playing from 90-200 Hz built with robust birch plywood and loaded with BMS 15s330’s this boxes provides enough smooth and warm kicks for all styles of music.

Polar Bear Scoop

The Polar Bear Scoops is the most northern scoop design. With its larger camber and long horn it plays both deeper and smoother than other scoop designs to date. Built with birch plywood and heavily reinforced and loaded with PD1850’s this boxes is a force to be recon with. Playing from 30 to 90 Hz giving a smooth and heavy bass working well in both large venues and outdoor locations.

Contact Us

Please contact us for rates and availability.

We offer equipment rental and event production services. Our Ivah Sound System has been featured in many festivals, club nights and underground parties in Finland and Estonia. With its unique look and awe-inspiring sound, it provides unique value for any party. Highly modular and mobile, the sound system is suited for both indoor and outdoor applications. Custom engineered and built in Helsinki, it delivers true hi-fidelity performance capable of filling venues up to 2000 capacity. We also provide DJ-services, photography, consultation and more.
