Jahtafari is a Roots Reggae Dubwise selector who grew up with reggae and the message of RastafarI. Ever since his father Iya Karna introduced him to reggae music he have been keen to spread the message forward. Jahtafari have found his own niche in the bass heavy Reggae, inspired by the great sound systems from England. He have since then taken on the project to build his own sound, and in 2010 together with his friends he built the now powerful Ivah Sound System. Jahtafari have performed on the same stage with artists such as Radikal Guru, Sammy Dread, King Shiloh and Tes La Rok. He have also selected tunes for reggae singers Salomo, Jah Vice, Cstar, Hosni, Fyah I and Ras Henry. Jahtafaris selection of rootical music have been heard in Greece, Scandinavia, Finland and Estonia. Together with Ivah Sound he has promoted numerous club events and happenings all over Helsinki as well as the rest of Finland.
