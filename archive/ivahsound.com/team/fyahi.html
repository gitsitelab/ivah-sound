Fyah I

MC Producer
4

Fyah-I is a young raggamuffin’ MC/SingJay that started Rapping and Singing at the age of 15 with the influence from RastafarI and the Jamaican Culture, Fyah-I also started Producing music with Adobe Audition, Cubase, FL Studio and other programs digitally, now he has build his little home studio including a AKAI APC20 live mixer and 2 MIDI keyboards, Focusrite soundcard and a studio microphone, with all that digital power he creates all kind of sounds going from Reggae to Dub, Steppers all the way to Deep Dubstep with the influences of something called SuomiSoundi. At the age of 16 he released his first demo on Youtube called Righteous Youths and has been developing his own style since that.

At the age of 16 he also had his first freestyle gigg in Uppsala Reggae Festival, when Junior Natural invited him on stage with Meditative Sound, after that he has had plenty of gigs all over Finland, and also performed at Scandinavian Reggae Festival with his bredrin from Ivah Sound.

Fyah-I is also the Co-Founder of Ivah Sound and has been helping to arrange parties all around Helsinki.

  • Hometown: Raseborg, Finland
  • Skills: MC DJ
