Autuas

DJ Producer
4

Autuas is a selector hailing out of Helsinki, Finland. He's a selector influenced by deep basslines, tuff riddims, dark atmospheres, digital beats and soulful melodies. He started collecting vinyls at the age of 14 and moved on to djing when he turned 18. Known from hes eclectic taste in music, he used to play and collect all range of genres from reggae to grime to punk, but nowadays he has channeled his passion towards jamaican influenced bass music. Together with hes crew Ivah Sound, a dj collective & sound system, they have organized monthly clubs and ug-parties around Helsinki area and toured in Europe and Scandinavia. Autuas plays a selection of roots, dub, steppas, jungle, grime and dubstep with exclusive Ivah Sound dubplates and unreleased tunes from upcoming producers. When hes not working on Ivah Sound and music affiliated projects, he works as a guidance counselor for disabled teens and adults.
