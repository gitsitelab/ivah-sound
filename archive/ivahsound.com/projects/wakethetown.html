WAKE THE TOWN Roots Meeting. This time residents JAH EQUITY and TULITAUKO Sound System meet up with the young lions outta Helsinki, the IVAH Sound System. Two full sound systems inna de arena, shaking the walls with special selections from the very roots to upfront to dubplate – roots & culture music at its best in a heavyweight sound system style!

Rastachef will provide ital food to keep mind and body strong and fit to skank all night long!

Liquid refreshment is provided by the Golden Classics Cafe. The venue is fully licensed and held in a truly vintage style featuring chill-out areas with flippers and pool table. The heart of the venue is the dancefloor, offering plenty of space for the headnodding and skanking massive and has proved to be an ideal spot to unfold the deepest bass and crispest heights of the mighty TULITAUKO Sound System. Arrive early for maximum pleasure!!!
