01 Who we are

Who we are.

Ivah Sound stands for unity, peace and love. Through conscious music, thundering bass and uplifting sessions, we share that message with the people who gather around our sound. We want our sessions to give people energy and the strength to stand firm in these times.

Since 2010, we've built our sound together, shaping the speakers, the music and the dances with our own hands. Each member brings something different, and our strength comes from what we do as a crew and with the people around us. That spirit runs through our own sessions and the collaborations that take us further afield.

From late nights in clubs to sessions deep in the Finnish forest, we bring a deep love of reggae and dub and an open mind for where the music can take us.

02 The sound system

Finnish design.
Finnish birch.

Our speakers are Finnish designs that we have built ourselves from Finnish birch plywood, and the system keeps developing. Every section works together as one instrument, from the deep sub bass to the highs.

  1. High frequencies

    Tweeters

    Beyma CP22 tweeters deliver the highest frequencies with clarity and presence at the top of the stack.

  2. Mid-high section

    MT112

    The vocal and musical centre of the system, projecting detail clearly across the dance.

  3. Upper bass

    Polar Kick

    The physical bridge between the sub-bass and midrange, built for impact and movement.

  4. Sub bass

    Polar Bear Scoops

    Horn-loaded sub-bass cabinets designed in Finland by Mikko Laurila and built by us, carrying the deepest frequencies.

03 History

Built by hand,
since 2010.

From the first self-built speakers to festival stages abroad. A short history of the sound, told through the sessions that shaped it.

  1. 2010

    A united sound system

    Ivah Sound forms in Helsinki in early 2010: a sound system and DJ collective of selectors and MCs, united by a passion for heavy bass music from roots reggae to steppers, jungle and drum & bass.

    In those early years we ran a hundred Tuesdays together with the SubzUp DJ collective, which led to our own clubs and parties all around Finland.

  2. Built

    The first generation in birch

    The cabinets of our first full stack are Finnish designs by Mikko Laurila, built by us from Finnish birch plywood:

    • Cheetah Horn — built around the JBL 2380A, covering the highs from 1 to 20 kHz.
    • MT121 — mids on Fane Colossus 12MB drivers, with a separate top of 2” coaxial drivers and horns.
    • Kick — a low-mid and upper bass section on BMS 15S330 drivers, playing 90 to 200 Hz.
    • Polar Bear Scoop — Mikko Laurila's scoop design for the sub bass, loaded with PD1850s, playing 30 to 90 Hz.

  3. 2013

    Jamaica in Suvilahti

    On 6 August 2013 we strung up the sound at Kalasatama's Konttiaukio for the Jamaica Independence celebration: good food, loud music and a street dance yard style in the open air. The same summer we played Scandinavian Reggae Festival in Copenhagen and supported Major Lazer with our sound system in Helsinki.

  4. 2014

    FRWRD

    Our own sound system club FRWRD opens at Ravintola Lämpö on 1 March 2014 with Panda Dub and Dan I Locks, made for passionate bass music and sound system lovers, with influences from the sound system cultures of Jamaica and the United Kingdom. Alpha Steppa, Tes La Rok and many more followed.

    The same year we met Jah Equity and Tulitauko Sound System at Wake The Town, took over Joensuu with Trey & Prospero and started a radio show on Helsinki Lähiradio.

  5. 2015

    Thousands in the park

    On 1 August 2015 we took over Kallio's Katri Vala park as one of the stages of Kallio Block Party. A couple of thousand people felt the bass for eight hours, old and young together in the sun. This is exactly why we made Ivah Sound in the first place: to bring people together with the power of music and love.

    Helsinki Dub Club with Tulitauko Sound, the vinyl-only Dub Union and FRWRD's move to the city centre followed, while Prospero and Trey's Top Shelf crew carried the drum & bass side.

  6. 2016

    Outlook, Croatia

    Our largest summer adventure so far: Ivah Sound on the Dub Smugglers stage at Outlook Festival in Croatia, after hosting the official Outlook Helsinki launch party with Drop Zone. Sound system culture also came to Kosmos Festival with Alpha Steppa and Tes La Rok.

    At home, FRWRD brought Deng Deng Hi-Fi from Malmö with Dan I Locks, King Ital and Puppa J for a two-sound meeting, and we started raising support to build new horn kicks designed here in Finland.

  7. Next

    Moving forward

    The sound keeps developing. Today's stack runs Beyma tweeters, MT112s, Polar Kicks and Polar Bear Scoops, and the sessions have moved from clubs to forests and festivals. We also share what we've learned through workshops and talks.

04 Ivah in the community

Made to bring people together.

We put care into every detail of our sessions, the sound, the music, the way the space looks and even the scents that fill it. Deep bass you feel through your body, lighting and decorations all shape the atmosphere. Together, they make each session an immersive experience.

We also share what we've learned over the years through workshops, talks and hands-on demonstrations, from building speakers and playing music to organising your own events.
